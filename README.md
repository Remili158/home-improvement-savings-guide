# Home Improvement Savings & Retail Discount Guide

A practical consumer guide to shopping policies, markdown schedules, and discount verification for major US hardware retailers.

## How Warehouse Markdown Cycles Work

Major home improvement retailers follow structured markdown schedules rather than random clearance discounts. Understanding how price tag endings operate helps homeowners and contractors purchase building supplies and power tools at their lowest historical prices.

### Key Clearance Price Tag Indicators
- **Standard Clearance Markdowns:** Most initial markdowns start at 25% to 33% off the original manufacturer retail price.
- **Final Price Markdowns:** Price tags ending in specific cent denominations (such as .06, .03, or .02) frequently signal final clearance inventory that will be liquidated or removed rather than marked down further.

## Applying Coupons and Split-Cart Strategies

When purchasing high-value tools alongside building materials (such as commodity lumber or electrical wire), shoppers often encounter coupon exclusions. 

Using a split-cart strategy allows you to apply percentage-off or dollar-off promo codes to eligible power tools and equipment in one transaction, while processing commodity or volume-priced materials separately to avoid discount cancellations.
