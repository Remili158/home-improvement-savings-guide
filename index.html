Home Improvement Savings & Retail Discount Guide

Renovating a home or stocking a workshop requires navigating retailer policies to ensure you pay the lowest possible prices on tools, lumber, and seasonal equipment.

Understanding Price Tag Clearance Cycles

Major home improvement chains do not mark down items randomly. Products follow a predictable progression from initial retail to clearance markdown tiers.

Pro Tip: Look closely at the cent endings on clearance tags. Specific endings (like .06 or .03) often indicate the final price threshold before items are phased out.
